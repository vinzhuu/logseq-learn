-
- 对于微信原生接口或组件:
	- 微信已经内置了一些无障碍特性.
	- 如 [wx.showModal](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/dev/api/ui/interaction/wx.showModal.html)、[navigation-bar](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/dev/component/navigation-bar.html) .
- 对于非原生内容:
	- 开发者可以添加无障碍标签进行开发.
	- 详见: [aria-component](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/dev/component/aria-component.html) .
